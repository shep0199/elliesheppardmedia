About

About Me

Creative Marketer · Visual Storyteller · Multimedia Creator

Hi, I'm Ellie! I'm an Ottawa-based creative who loves bringing ideas to life, from the first brainstorm to the final edit.

I love working with small businesses, entrepreneurs, and nonprofits to create content that tells their stories and connects with their audiences. Whether I'm developing a creative strategy, planning a shoot, capturing photos and videos, or bringing everything together in post-production, I genuinely enjoy being part of the entire process. For me, creative marketing is about more than making something look good. It's about understanding the story behind a brand and finding the best way to share it.

I graduated from Queen's University with a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Film and Media and a minor in Sociology, where I also led marketing and communications for a student society representing over 14,000 students. I'm now studying Interactive Media Management at Algonquin College, expanding my skills in web design, UX/UI, and digital media while continuing to work with clients as a freelancer.

I love collaborating with people who are passionate about what they do, and I'm always excited to explore new ideas, creative challenges, and opportunities to tell meaningful stories.
